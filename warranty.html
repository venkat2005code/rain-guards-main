Atelier Expertise

Care & Maintenance Protocol

Extend the pristine condition of your canopy and hardware through proper seasonal maintenance.

01

Drying Protocol

Always leave your umbrella open in a well-ventilated area until completely dry before storing in its sleeve to prevent fabric degradation.

02

Coating Revival

Restore water-repellency every two years with a gentle application of low-heat hairdryer treatment on the exterior canopy surface.

03

Handle Nourishment

For wooden handles, apply natural beeswax balm annually to preserve grain richness and prevent natural weathering cracks.

04

Professional Tuning

Never force a stuck latch. Send your piece to our atelier for complimentary tension recalibration whenever needed.
