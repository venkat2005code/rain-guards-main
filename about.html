Our Story

From a Single Workbench to Four Capitals

Aurumbrella began as a one-room repair shop on Burlington Gardens, where our founder re-ribbed the umbrellas of Mayfair's tailors and bankers. Frustrated by the fragility of what crossed his bench, he set out to build a canopy that would never need repairing at all.

That standard still guides every piece we make. Ribs are cut from aerospace-grade carbon, canopies are woven from our proprietary hydrophobic silk, and every ferrule and handle is finished by hand before it leaves the atelier. Today our work travels from London to Milan, Tokyo and New York, but every design still starts at that first bench.

Milestones

A Hundred Years in the Rain

  1. 1924

    The first Aurumbrella workshop opens on Burlington Gardens, Mayfair.

  2. 1958

    Introduction of the signature solid-brass ferrule, still used across the collection today.

  3. 1997

    Carbon-fibre rib architecture launches after three years of wind-tunnel development.

  4. 2016

    Ateliers open in Milan and Tokyo, followed by the New York studio on Madison Avenue.

  5. 2024

    Centenary year. Archival Edition No. 04 released with a lifetime structural guarantee.
